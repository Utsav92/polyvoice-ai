Caveats

  • 30 single-speaker English sentences from the same dataset; this measures intelligibility, not naturalness. Nobody has listened to the full set.
  • FastSpeech2 at 6k scored WER 0.036 vs 0.052 at 40k; the gap is a few words and within noise for n=30.
  • Audio comes from the pretrained Vocos vocoder (MIT); only the acoustic model is ours.
